# DESIGN.md — Leticia Angeline Portfolio Design System (Impeccable Edition)

## Design Philosophy: Japanese Editorial Craft & Warm Sumi Paper
A refined, sophisticated editorial portfolio inspired by Japanese publication design (Midori MD, Hobonichi, Kinfolk) and modern engineering craft (Linear, Stripe). Restraint in color, elegance in typography, and perfection in spatial rhythm.

## Color Tokens (OKLCH / Hex Mappings)
- **Surfaces:**
  - Page Ground: `#faf7f2` (Warm Vanilla Rice Paper)
  - Card Solid: `#ffffff` (Pure 100% Opaque Card Stock)
  - Card Sub-surface: `#f6f1e8` (Warm Tinted Linen)
  - Terminal Inset: `#1a1614` (Deep Sumi Charcoal)
- **Typography & Inks:**
  - Headline & Lead: `#1e1815` (Deep Sumi Black, AAA contrast)
  - Body Text: `#382e29` (Warm Espresso Ink)
  - Muted / Meta: `#6e5e54` (Warm Stone)
  - Faint / Footnotes: `#9c8c82`
- **Brand Accents:**
  - Camellia Rose: `#d94e6b` (Primary brand accent, CTAs & active states)
  - Hanko Vermilion: `#c94a29` (Subtle seal/stamp accent)
  - Matcha Green: `#3e7b56` (Verified metrics & success indicators)
  - Slate Ink: `#2c3e50` (Secondary technical badges)
  - Border Hairlines: `#e5dbcf` (1.5px subtle warm rule)
  - Active Border: `#d94e6b`

## Typography Hierarchy
- **Editorial Headlines (Display):** `'Playfair Display', serif` (Weights: 600, 700) for hero name, section titles, and project titles.
- **Interface & Body:** `'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif` (Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700) for readable, crisp paragraphs and buttons.
- **Code & Metrics:** `'Space Mono', monospace` (Weights: 400, 700) for metrics, timestamps, and code snippets.

## Layout & Spatial Constraints
- Container max-width: `1120px` (optical compactness).
- Consistent spacing scale: `8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`.
- Solid 100% opaque cards with 1.5px warm hairline borders and soft tactile shadows.
- No rainbow stripes, no cartoon emojis, no faux-wood washi tape. Pure graphic craft.
